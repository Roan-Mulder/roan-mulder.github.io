About Me

I am a game design student at Breda University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands. I specialize in level design within Unreal Engine. I work best on platformer projects in both 3D and 2.5D, but I have experience in a variety of genres. Outside of level design I have some experience in writing dialogue and implementing audio within projects, as well as writing simple music for game jams.

I chose to do level design because I enjoy creating spaces and worlds that players can explore and move through. Great level design can make a game truly stand out, and I aspire to create spaces that are memorable and fun to move around in. I also enjoy working in team environments and solving problems together with others. I have received plenty of positive feedback from the people I have worked with, particularly on my flexibility in adapting to what a project needs and on being open to listening to every discipline on the team. As a level designer I especially enjoy working closely with artists, so that my levels can support their artistic vision.
